==========================================
API端点概览
==========================================

1. 发送消息（非流式）
   POST /api/v1/chat/message
   请求: {"content": "消息内容", "conversation_id": null}
   响应: {"conversation_id": 123, "message": {...}, "usage": {...}}

2. 发送消息（流式SSE）
   POST /api/v1/chat/stream
   请求: {"content": "消息内容"}
   响应: Server-Sent Events 流

3. 获取对话列表
   GET /api/v1/chat/conversations?page=1&page_size=20
   响应: {"total": 100, "items": [...]}

4. 获取对话详情
   GET /api/v1/chat/conversations/123
   响应: {"id": 123, "messages": [...]}

5. 删除对话
   DELETE /api/v1/chat/conversations/123
   响应: {"success": true}

6. 添加反馈
   POST /api/v1/chat/feedback
   请求: {"message_id": 456, "rating": 5, "feedback": "..."}
   响应: {"message_id": 456, "rating": 5}
